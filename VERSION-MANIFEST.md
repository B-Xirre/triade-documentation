## 0.42.0 release summary

### P12-A — Faculty entitlement and acquisition identity — 22 September 2026

P12-A closes without closing `◇P12`. Entitlement attaches only to the four immutable base Faculty identities. A composite Faculty profile is an execution-authorization profile, never a separately acquired identity or entitlement. Arcana && Mudra therefore requires possessed Arcana and possessed Mudra; Mudra && Mudra requires one possessed Mudra plus two functional, unoccupied hands, each providing a distinct functional finger hook.

P·2.3c adds two authoritative grains and one projection. `build_faculty_instructions` records immutable build-level `confer`, `unlock` or `acquire` instructions. `actor_faculty_acquisitions` records authoritative character-state acquisition facts. Generated `actor_faculty_entitlements` folds those facts with current Lineage grants and remains read-only.

Lifecycle facts do not collapse. `conferred` is automatic possession; `unlocked` is eligibility rather than possession; `acquired` is persisted character state. `possessed` derives from conferred or acquired and is not stored separately. `available` remains a later runtime derivation and is never persisted as entitlement state.

An acquisition normally survives loss of the source that unlocked it. A source may revoke it only through an explicit leased/revocable contract naming the revocation rule. This keeps temporary equipment, effects and changing prerequisites from silently deleting permanent progression while preserving a deliberate route for temporary entitlements.

P12-B/C remain the sole Session B design work: complete availability gates and deterministic evaluation order, including Triade position and accessible floor. Tarot Session C1 remains after all of Session B. The technical specification records a schema/migration handoff, not implemented relations. The suite grows from 204 to **205 rules — 135 Critical / 56 High / 14 Medium**. Design-stream instructions advance to v1.29; technical-stream instructions remain content-aligned to v0.38.0 under pending counter-patch CP-5.1.

### P13 — normalized Lineage/Physique and Somatic authorization — 20 September 2026

`◈P13` closes in P·2.3a with twelve normalized relations: Lineage identity/revision, allowed size and typical marker, nine conserved stat offsets, non-typical Physique pairs, node profiles and entries, enemy-floor profiles, innate grants, Technique bindings, binding source nodes, permitted Payloads and Payload support nodes. Somatic becomes the fourth Faculty family beside Arcana, Mudra and Psyche. It authorizes deliberate natural-node Techniques, but contributes no independent footprint or delivery hook: the active bound anatomical node remains the source. Faculty lifecycle distinguishes **conferred** (automatically possessed), **unlocked** (eligible for acquisition), **acquired** (possessed), and derived **available** (currently usable). Availability is never persisted as independent truth; destroyed source/support nodes, prerequisites and cooldowns can make an acquired Faculty unavailable.

The Venomous Hobgoblin example keeps natural combat and venom production separate. `venomous_hobgoblin.natural_combat@1` binds Bite to teeth, Punch to hand and Kick to foot; Bite may carry Poison with the venom gland as support, producing the derived rendition Poisonous Bite. `venomous_hobgoblin.venom_production@1` binds Apply Venom to the venom gland and permits Poison. The malformed `venom_gland → Poison` source-node row is rejected: Poison is a Payload, not a node. Rend remains a weapon Technique and is not lineage-granted.

Cooldowns declare the identities they check and start. A base Technique cooldown gates all its derived renditions; a rendition cooldown gates only that child by default; a provider cooldown gates new provision/application; a Faculty-wide cooldown is an exceptional shared lock. Therefore Rend on cooldown makes Poisonous Rend unavailable, Poisonous Rend on cooldown may leave plain Rend available, and a regenerating venom gland prevents new gland venom while an existing transferred coating may remain usable and plain Rend remains unaffected.

## 0.34.0 release summary

**AP stops being a resource.**

### The correctness constant went first, deliberately

`S-P01` is closed at **`FIXED_POINT_SCALE = 12 000`** — **derived**, being the smallest scale that exactly represents the fractions this corpus actually uses: `1/3 = 4 000_q`, `1/12`, `0.05`, `0.25`, `0.35`, `0.45`, and `1/6` and `0.1` besides. Sequenced first so CR-11's schemas could not inherit an unresolved correctness constant. **The scale governs persisted fractional state only** — `world_tick`, tick durations, AP rates and `u8` channels are ordinary integers, stated as a negative because *"we use fixed point"* invites someone to store time in it.

### AP as rate

```
action_duration_ticks = effective_AP_cost × (60 / current_AP_rate)
```

Band **2–6**, base **4**. Every legal rate divides 60 exactly, so no action needs a fractional tick. K·3.1's *one activation per round* and K·3.4's pool are **retired**; the 60-tick band is a cadence and refresh boundary, not a grant.

**"Unused AP is lost" was not replaced — it dissolved.** That rule existed to stop a player pre-loading a create→read→convert chain. **A rate cannot be pre-loaded**, so the exploit closes by construction rather than by prohibition. The same argument strengthens K·8: Watch's *"not held-back AP"* becomes **unanswerable**, because there is no balance to hold back. The counter/decline branches, Provoke and the Riposte ladder survive the rewrite untouched.

### Readiness keeps its conclusion and loses its premise

It was ordinal *because activations were capped at one per round* — a premise this release deletes. The replacement is stronger and is a **type** argument rather than an arithmetic one: **Readiness is never converted into ticks or into rate.** It orders nodes and breaks same-tick ties, and **its effect saturates at first position among otherwise simultaneous eligible nodes**, so it cannot manufacture action frequency however the timeline evolves (**K-H2**).

### Coupling, restated

**No Triade corner contributes an additive or multiplicative term to AP rate** (**K-C12**). Momentum gives Readiness rank; Mind gives effective cost and therefore duration; **Form gives the minimum floor — a clamp, not a contribution.** Only authored effects such as Haste/Slow move `current_AP_rate`, inside 2–6. ## 0.27.0 release summary

**◇H15 stood open from 0.13.0 because the question was mis-stated.** It asked *who supplies a player's chassis*, as if a system were missing. Nothing was missing: `[Chassis]` already named a body template, and what it lacked was a name for the thing that makes chassis plural. That is **lineage**, and the two are one record.

### What was authored

**T·A4.8a — Lineage, the other stat lens.** Class shapes which stats you develop; lineage sets which you were born with. Both are `±` masks over the A4.1 grid, neither is a term in the field formula, and both sum to zero. Lineage sits beside class because **class is already that schema** — `offset: 9-stat vector, MUST sum to zero`.

**The offset is measured at the typical size.** A lineage flags exactly one size as ordinary, and it need not be Average: a Rat's typical is Small, a Troll's is Large. A lineage vector therefore always describes *an ordinary member*, wherever ordinary sits. The typical row carries no physique modifiers — it is the zero point, not a step.

**E·F.1 — the size ladder.** `Small → Average → Large → Giant`, a per-lineage subset, at most two additive integer pairs per non-typical size, each summing to zero. Two pairs is a ceiling by rule: beyond it, size stops being a lens on a lineage and becomes a second lineage.

### The invariant this buys

**Every stat source in the game conserves.** Lineage, class, sub-class and physique all sum to zero, and two zero-sum offsets sum to zero-sum. **No combination of birth, training and size produces a power gain — only a shape.** It is the stat-space form of the conservation the dot already obeys under A2.4.

**So a Rat and a Troll have the same stat total.** Not a gap to patch later: T·F.0 already defines the four tiers *"by which loop stages they possess, **not by stat size**"*. Lineage is shape, tier is capability, nothing is scale. A design that later wants a numerically larger creature must reopen this rather than work around it.

### Three names, and why they are these names

**Lineage**, because `[Race]` collides with the corpus's *damage race / kill race* sense — seven live uses including the locked non-goal `⦻H3`. That objection was raised when ◇H15 was written and has not changed.

**Typical**, never *standard*: **Standard** is enemy Tier 2 *and* the shield sub-family. It would have been that word's third sense.

**`[Average size]`**, replacing `[Medium size]` — undefined by the ladder, and `Medium` was locked two versions ago as a weight class. Two collisions in the corpus's only authored physique value.

### Additive integers, not multipliers

A `×0.9 / ×1.1` pair conserves only when both base stats are equal — it leaks the moment a character has more Strength than Finesse. **P-C2** bars floats from persisted state because **W-C10** bars float-derived values from the proof digest. Every conserving layer here is an additive integer vector, and these are too.

The technical stream's instructions join the set as **`TRIADE-Technical_Stream_Project_Instructions-[V_e_r].md`**. Both are tracked, archived and swept like any document. Neither holds design authority; neither may originate a rule.

**Each carries two version numbers, on purpose.** An internal `Instructions version` on its own sequence, and the design-set version in the filename. The filename version is not a claim about content — it is a visual confirmation that the file was co-authored during that bump, readable without opening it.

### `AUTHORED DESIGN DECISION`

The technical stream may resolve a design question when implementation cannot proceed without one. That resolution is now a **working decision**: legal to implement against, recorded as an intake item of that type, and **non-authoritative until the central process disposes it `authored`**. Technical documents must label it *authored-but-not-yet-centralised* and may not cite it as an existing rule — **`P-C10`**, Critical.

**This does not weaken the 0.17.0 authority lock.** The lock says a non-design document may not *originate* a rule. It never said what happens when implementation must choose anyway. So the choice got made and recorded nowhere — which is precisely what `L-M1` and `L-M2` looked like eight versions later. The state now has a name, a record and a rule.

### Changes flowing the other way

The central process governs the technical instructions but does not author them. When it needs a change there it issues a **counter-patch** — a tracked record, one item per change, retiring only when a replacement technical file lands carrying them. **Five items shipped with this release.**

One exception, stated so it is not stretched: repointing a filename *this process itself renamed* is a cross-reference sweep, not an authored change.

**CP5 is the honest one.** The technical file's filename now reads 0.21.0 while its internal *Aligned to Triade* still reads 0.20.0 — because a filename is a marker the central process can set, and *aligned to* is a claim that the stream has re-proofed its assumptions. The central process cannot make that claim on the stream's behalf. **The two disagreeing is the correct state until the stream closes it.**

## 0.15.0 release summary

**A tenth document: `P` — Content Pipeline & Data Model.** Created by reconciling two independently written studies — a full domain model and a scoped storage-technology sweep. They overlapped only on the storage layer, and **agreed there**.

### Why a document rather than sections in M

Five consumers: **M** (equipment, affix, faculty model; generation; fixtures), **T** (trace instrumentation, Trace Signature, redundancy gate), **K** (every combat action is a trace row), **H** (per-delta attribution for wounds, integrity, ward), **E** (enemies share the schema). E·0's test — *"a section consumed by three documents is a subsystem"* — and at creation P is larger than four existing documents.

**It is data architecture, not game design.** Nothing in P decides how the game plays, which is what makes it separable.

### The five lifecycle objects

The corpus had been conflating five things that behave differently:

```
Equipment definition → Generated roll → Owned item instance → Equipped loadout snapshot → Attributed combat trace
```

A trace points at the exact revision used at the time, so **a balance patch creates a new revision rather than rewriting history.** A player's sword may be Fractured while the sword *definition* is untouched.

### Storage — the convergent half

**Canonical JSON is the source of truth; DuckDB is a rebuildable artefact.** Both studies reached this independently, and it reproduces M·Part 4 item 1 (*"content is data, never code"*) which neither set out to satisfy.

The build is **one-directional — text → database, never the reverse.** `.duckdb` and Parquet are binary and not git-diffable, and DuckDB has no row-level diff. If anyone edits the database directly, diffability and reproducibility are both lost.

**DuckDB is pinned to the v1.4 LTS line.** v2.0 lands September 2026, the same month the LTS expires. A design document naming a database without naming a version has a hidden dependency (`◇P1`).

**Never commit raw Parquet to git.** Sim output regenerates from seeds — version the seeds, config and sim code.

### Fixed-point, not floats

**All persisted simulation and trace state uses fixed-point integers.** This is not a preference: **W-C10** already bars float-derived values from the proof digest, and float accumulation across a 10,000-seed sweep is not reproducible across platforms. The scale is a versioned constant, never an undocumented multiplier.

### Per-delta attribution

One row per state change, carrying `source_kind`, `contribution_role`, `rule_id`, `opening_provenance` and `is_counterfactual`. That last field marks paired runs, which is what makes **delta signatures** computable — a modifier's signature is the difference between traces with and without it.

It answers the question the design actually needs: *was this item strong because of raw damage, extra vocabulary, displacement, Opening creation, credit conversion, mitigation, or synergy?*

### The agent's query path is read-only

Text-to-SQL on a schema this shallow is reliable — roughly 94–95% under realistic review, near 76% on strict benchmarks whose annotations are themselves disputed. **Read-only makes a wrong query harmless, which is cheaper than making it correct.** Content writes pass through record validation (Pydantic v2) and table validation (Pandera), never through the agent's SQL tool.

### ◈M10 closed — P·Part 10

The fixture set is now records, not a specification. Two corrections to K·15, which was written at 0.9.0 and predates the faculty channel:

**None of its three loadouts could cast.** Maul, daggers, sword-and-shield each occupy both hands, so the somatic gate — and Fantasy 3, which exists to validate it — had no fixture.

**The carrier is a free off-hand, not a wand.** Five builds now: Striker, Controller, Technical, **Trickster** (single dagger) and **War Priest** (one-handed mace). The last two carry `[Mudra]`, `[Psyche]`, `[Arcana]` and Divine purge between them.

**A Canine chassis buys three things at once** — the only non-humanoid template (exercising `◇E5`, partially unblocking `◇V1` and `◇G6`), `[Innate]` slots derived from a quadruped template (proving `H-C7`), and a bite gated on jaw and teeth, the same nodes that deny `[Arcana]`. One wound, two denials, no new rule.

**The negative cases are fixtures too:** `[Arcana]`+`[Arcana]` impossible, three sources illegal, `[Mudra]` refused on a two-hander. A rule nothing fails is a rule nothing tests.

### Naming

**`extraction` rejected for the third time.** It appeared as a trace `outcome` value; it is reserved to Zone extraction (W§12), and a band leaves through a `[Band-End Portal]`. `band_end_exit` adopted.

### Suite

**133 rules — 73 Critical, 46 High, 14 Medium.** Twelve added, all in P.

## 0.14.0 release summary

**Gate 0 passed on three of four criteria; the run contract is locked.** No new document — the edits land in M, W, E and L, so the set stays at nine.

### The storey is the pacing unit, not the Delve level

Both counts were already locked and nobody had multiplied them:

| Band | Levels *(S-W01)* | `N` *(W·5.8)* | **Storeys** | Duration |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A 1→3 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 30–45 min |

| | | | **18** | |

Two independently written Gate 0 studies derived the same contradiction with the same arithmetic and the same citation.

### ×9 career, and the constant that falls out of it

Power compounds across bands — gear persists through the town return — so the multiple is a **career** quantity, not a per-run one. Uniform growth over 18 storeys:

**A ×1.44 · B ×4.33 · C ×1.44 · career ×9.0**

Band C equals band A because both are three storeys. That removes the terminal anomaly at its root rather than patching around it.

At **two major acquisitions per storey** — 36 career, sitting on the best-documented comparator figure — per-pick value is `9^(1/36)` = **6.3%, constant across the whole career**. Uniform per-pick value is what makes loot legible: an item is worth the same wherever it drops. It is derived, not chosen, and it disciplines the affix pool — at two acquisitions per storey nothing small is ever perceptible.

**T-C3 keeps the Triade out of it.** `Σ Φ_base` is conserved, so 0.9 spans item power, vocabulary breadth and `[Skill Level]` — and two of those three are breadth, not magnitude.

**Purchased Delve access is no longer lost to ordinary death.** Collapse previously had six value channels answering *"what did death cost?"* at once — run gear, unspent Grounding, `[Modification Ceiling]`, access, a carried `[Relic]`, and persistent wounds with paid recovery. Each is followable and the aggregate is not. Access is now what a checkpoint permanently buys.

Bands A and B lock by clearing the boss and leaving through a **`[Band-End Portal]`** — no partial lock, no mid-band exit. **Band C alone** may carry one interior exit between storeys and requires save-and-resume.

Written rather than implied, because an unstated contradiction inside a locked section is exactly the failure ◈T6 took two versions to close. The exemption is narrow by construction: band C only, between storeys, never mid-storey.

`E(x) = P(x) × (0.92 + 0.18x)` — an **encounter budget** covering durability, action quality, behaviour tags, spatial pressure and resource denial, not a health multiplier. With a ×9 career this is what stops cleared bands trivialising on replay. Neither W nor E states how it is implemented: **◇W17** and **◇E11**, a partner pair.

### Occult load — the ratchet

In-combat purge clears the encounter state. It does **not** clear the exposure. Corruption and displacement accrue to a cumulative **occult load** whether or not they are purged, converting past threshold to ward scars that only the town rite clears. **In-combat purge is tactically complete and strategically partial** — which is what stops a Divine caster making H·8.2's rite redundant.

## 0.12.0 release summary

**The dungeon builder gained a vertical dimension and a progression layer.** Nine documents now; **G** (Tile Pipeline) is new.

### Vertical structure

*Floor* is reserved to its Triade barycentric meaning — 103 uses in T, 39 in W, **none geometric**. Both candidate names for dungeon geometry were rejected as L-M2 collisions and replaced:

| Term | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| **`[Deck]`** | A stacked traversable sheet within one combat room; shares the room's 2–4 zone overlay |
| **`[Storey]`** | One generated bundle within a Delve level; own zone overlays, joined by `[Vertical Portal]` |

**One Delve level = one dungeon of `N` storeys, charged as one descent.** `N` is derived from the locked 3/6/1 Stratum structure — 1 / 2 / 3 by band — and fixed, with no archetype override. **`N` partitions the level's space budget rather than multiplying it**, which is what leaves S-W01 and S-W02 needing no re-sweep, and what keeps risk-per-descent constant so S-W03's domain stays stable.

A storey transition is a **streaming boundary, not an economic one**. Stated as a negative because it would otherwise be relitigated.

### Progression placement — a new pipeline stage

W§13 gains **Stage 5a**, a dungeon-scope reservation pass running before tiling. It exists because the **descent unlock point** — locked by W§5.3, mandatory in every level 1–9 — had no stage that placed it. It now covers `[Secret]` gates, the descent point, boss anchors, `[Region Vocabulary]` teaching nodes and `[Relic]`s under one proof.

Dungeon scope, not storey scope: a per-storey pass could place a key behind its own gate and the storey's internal reachability proof would pass.

### New vocabulary

| Term | What |
| --- | --- |
| **`[Secret]`** | A storey gate. Three **monotonic** resolution verbs — *operated*, *picked up*, *destroyed* — so the soft-lock class is closed by construction, not proofed against |
| **`[Switch]`** | The latching state-change object. Irreversible, non-cover, indestructible on mandatory paths. *Lever* stays reserved to K-C8's tempo sense |
| **`[Relic]`** | Carried out of a Location by the verb *collected*. **Access only** — never Grounding, Ceiling, floor budget or stats. Lost on collapse, re-placed same-Location, secured by reaching town |
| **`[Incursion]`** | A dungeon belonging to no Location, Relic-gated. Unrestricted depth, no progression, saturating item level |

**`[Secret]` is decoupled from inventory** and held in a run-scoped key register. This closes every loss path at once — full inventory, drop, sale, salvage — and lets storeys ship without waiting on the undesigned `[Inventory]` system.

**Why `[Incursion]` rather than a Relic-gated Stratum entrance:** a gated Stratum entrance could substitute for the Stratum lock payment, making the Relic `[Imprint]` under another name. An Incursion has no payment to substitute for. The bypass risk ceases to exist rather than being proofed against.

### Zone extraction

**`complex_room` exempts the 2-zone floor, never the 4-zone ceiling** (position A2). K·4 states flatly that each room holds two to four zones and carries no exception; A2 means K stands unamended and the substrate stays uniform. What an authored set-piece needs is zone *identity*, not zone *quantity*.

The ceiling cannot fail by construction — a zone cannot span decks (scalar `elevation_band`, W-C19), so merging always reduces to one zone per deck. **The failure is the floor**, resolved by furnishing repair → reseed → **demotion to non-combat**. Demotion always succeeds, so extraction degrades rather than erroring, with no human in the loop.

`high_ground` is confirmed a **derived tag**, not an elevation reading.
